### The through-line

I started at **Berklee** (Electronic Production & Design — essentially programming —
with a minor in electronics & acoustics, *cum laude*), came up through **C, DSP and
Csound** in the lineage of the people who wrote the book on it, and carried that
signal-processing instinct forward: music-information retrieval and embedded work, then
early machine learning, then — chasing *continuous, defensible data* — into
**quantitative finance**, and now into **games and audio R&D**. Different domains, one
habit of mind: model the system honestly, then make it fast, cheap, and real.

### How I work
- **Own the metal.** A private RTX-3090 GPU mesh on a Tailscale network — fine-tuning,
  VRAM-safe serving, cross-site redundancy. Private models, cost-optimized, no cloud lock-in.
- **Eval-first & honest.** My methodology work is citable, reproducible, and candid about
  where it stops — negative results included. I'd rather show I understand the ideas than
  hide the gold.
- **Polymath by design.** Audio · DSP · acoustics · quantitative markets · AI · data
  engineering · games — the range is the point.
